## Tag along · [Your city] · [Date] at [time] · [Venue, address, directions] · Free, donations welcome · [Your contact]

Let's meet and sing barbershop together! Mostly tags, maybe a song, maybe something someone makes up on the spot. **Everyone is welcome.** High voice or low voice, first time singing or forty years in a choir, there's a part for you. You don't need to read music.

## What is barbershop?

[Barbershop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbershop_music) is four-part close harmony, sung without instruments. You've probably heard it without knowing the name: there's a quartet in [The Music Man](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nO0DFttQoJc), one in [Scrubs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hN8vA_bulss), and one with [Jimmy Fallon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-4FtBjjelA). The four parts are tenor, lead, baritone, and bass, and the lead sings the melody. The music is full of seventh chords, and when they're in tune they ring so hard you can hear notes nobody is singing. It takes practice to get there, which is half the fun. Deke Sharon calls it [the martial arts of a cappella singing](https://web.archive.org/web/20191209055218/http://www.casa.org/content/acamartialart).

## What are tags?

A tag is the big finish of a barbershop song, usually just a few bars long. We teach them by ear, one part at a time. It takes about twenty minutes to learn one, and then the whole room sings it together in four-part harmony.

## Can I listen to more barbershop?

Yes! We made a [playlist on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/3lTvmmXwszFdAIgxAbUCBn?si=C1_sEaL4Tb6YicXAb4ezKg) as a small introduction to the style.

## What do I need to prepare?

Nothing! We'll have sheet music printed out, and a toy piano in case we need it.

## What do I need to bring?

You & your voice! Maybe some water. Maybe a friend.

## Who's behind this?

A small group of people who love singing barbershop together. Our events follow a [code of conduct](/code-of-conduct/), and if anything feels wrong, you can talk to [contact person] at any time.
