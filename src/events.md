# Upcoming events

Here's where you can tag along right now:

## Berlin

[Capital Chords](https://capitalchords.de/?p=113) hosts a free, drop-in *Tag along* every **2nd and 4th Monday of the month, 19:00**, at the Philipp-Melanchthon-Kirche, Kranoldstr. 16, 12051 Berlin-Neukölln (near S+U Hermannstraße). Bilingual English/German, donations welcome, no experience needed. Their page lists all dates.

## Bern

The Swiss Association of Barbershop Singers hosts a free afternoon of tag singing: [Tag Along Day 2026](https://barbershopharmony.ch/event/tag-along-day-2026/), Saturday June 13, 2026, 13:00–17:00 at PROGR (Kleine Bühne), Waisenhausplatz 30, Bern. Tags, a workshop, and an apéro.
