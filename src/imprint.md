## Privacy

This site tries to know as little about you as possible:

- **No cookies, no tracking, no analytics.**
- **Fonts are self-hosted**, nothing is loaded from Google or any other third party.
- The site is served by [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/) ([privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/)). Like any web server, Netlify processes your IP address in order to deliver the pages, and keeps short-lived server logs.
- If you use the [contact form](/contact/), the data you enter (name, email, message) is transmitted to and stored by Netlify Forms so that we can read and answer your message. We don't use it for anything else.
